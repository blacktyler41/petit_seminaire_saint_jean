    About Us

    Who we are and what we stand for

    Our seminary is a centre of academic excellence and spiritual growth, preparing young Catholic men to serve in their community and church with outstanding academics, social and spiritual values.

    We nurture students to discover their vocations and talents, helping them grow and positively impact the wider world.

    The minor seminary emphasizes discipline, personal responsibility, prayer and community life, and acts as a bridge to the major seminary.

    The curriculum combines standard high school subjects with religious education, with special focus on Latin and Music.

    Our Patron Saint

    Saint John is a symbol of courage, purity and unwavering faith. He inspires our students to grow in integrity, compassion and resilience.

    His life teaches us that greatness lies in the strength of character and the courage to stand for what is right.
