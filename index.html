    About Us

    Who we are and what we stand for

    Our seminary is a centre of academic excellence and spirtual growth, preparing young catholic men to serve in their community and church with outstanding academics, social and spiritual values.

    We nurture students to discover their vocations and talents, helping them grow and positively impact the wider world.

    Minor seminary empasizes discipline, personal responsibility, prayer, community life and act as a bridge to major seminary,

    The curriculum combines standard high school subjects with religious education, with special focus on Latin and Music

    Our Patron Saint

    Saint John, is a symbol of courage, purity, and unwavering faith. He inspires our students to grow in integrity, compassion, and resilience.

    His life teaches us that greatness by the strength of character and the courage to stand for what is right.

    Admissions

    Join our seminary and begin your journey of excellence and faith

    Requirements

    To be admitted to Minor Seminary Saint John Nkumba, candidates must be Catholic and have received the sacraments of Baptism, Eucharist, and Confirmation, verified by their parish office. Candidates should also demonstrate good discipline, strong academic performance, and a sincere desire for spiritual and priestly formation.

    Application Process

    Parents or guardians must register Catholic candidates at their parish at least two years before joining Minor Seminary Saint John Nkumba. During this period, candidates undergo preparation and selection assessments leading to the final entrance examination.

    Contact Admissions

    For further details about enrollment and guidance, please contact our administration office. Our staff will be pleased to provide the information and support needed to guide you through the admission process.
