About Us

Who we are and what we stand for

Our seminary is a centre of academic excellence and spirtual growth,preparing young catholic men to serve in their community and church with outstanding academics, social and spiritual values.

We are committed to nurturing young catholic men to draw out their vocations and talents to enable them to flourish and make positive impact on othersand wider world

Our Patron Saint

Saint John, is a symbol of courage, purity,and unwavering faith. He inspires our students to grow in integrity, compassion, and resilience.

His life teaches us that greatness by the strength of character and the courage to stand for what is right.

Admissions

Join our seminary and begin your journey of excellence and faith

Requirements

to be accepted to enter Minor seminary, saint John NKUMBA you must be a catholic christian with basic sacraments means that of baptism, eucharist an confirmation, and this must be approved by the parish office. Also candidates must demonstrate discipline, strong academic performance, and a desire to grow spiritually.

Application Process

for young catholic men who wish to enter the minor seminary saint John NKUMBA, their parents must first register their children to their parish two years before intering Minor seminary saint John NKUMBA. so that to be prepared for entry examination. Durig that process there are different selection exams which prepare them to do entry examination after two years.
